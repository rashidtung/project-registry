# Pakistan FBR individual income tax

Help the taxpayer prepare a source-backed return for the requested tax year in IRIS. Establish the tax year, residency, income sources, return type, and current official deadline before calculating or entering figures. For a normal tax year, the period ends 30 June and is named for that calendar year; check for a special tax year or extension rather than assuming one. Use the current FBR form, notifications, and IRIS state for year-specific fields and rates. Do not carry forward rates or filing dates from an older guide.

## Intake and evidence

Ask for records in manageable groups. First obtain the prior filed return and wealth statement, the relevant year's income and tax deduction evidence, bank and investment statements, and opening/closing asset and liability details. Then request records for any applicable rental, business, property, securities, foreign, gift, inheritance, or other transactions. Ask which categories do not apply. Do not request passwords, PINs, or OTPs in chat.

For Tax Year 2026 under a normal tax year, collect evidence for 1 July 2025 through 30 June 2026 and balances at 30 June 2025 and 30 June 2026. Keep the income period and balance dates distinct. Capture source, date, amount, owner, and tax already withheld or paid for each relevant item. Treat FBR Maloomat and prefilled IRIS data as comparison evidence; reconcile discrepancies against primary records before using them.

## Prepare and check

Build an inventory of income, taxable gains, exemptions, deductions/credits, withholding and advance tax, assets, liabilities, transfers, and personal expenses. Map each item to the current IRIS form and preserve a trace to its source document. Separate asset values from income and cash movements; avoid counting inter-account transfers as income. Reconcile changes in wealth to income, expenses, gifts/inheritance, disposals, and borrowing. Surface unexplained differences, uncertain classifications, and missing evidence before a filing recommendation. Have the taxpayer or a qualified tax professional decide material judgment calls.

Review the computed tax, credits, payments, wealth reconciliation, and IRIS draft together. Verify successful filing only when the income return and wealth statement appear under Completed Tasks and retain the acknowledgement. Do not treat a saved draft or a clicked Submit button as proof of filing. Submission is a consequential external action: show the completed figures and unresolved issues for review, then act only with the user's explicit authorization and any action-time confirmation required by the UI tool.
